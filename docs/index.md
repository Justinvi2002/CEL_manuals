# CEL Engineering Manual

Welcome to the **CEL Engineering Manual**.

This manual provides practical engineering guidance, design rules, selection criteria, and technical reference information for electrical switchboard design and engineering.

The information is organised by manufacturer and equipment type to allow quick access to the required technical guidance.

## Purpose

The purpose of this manual is to:

- Standardise engineering and design practices.
- Provide clear equipment selection guidance.
- Record established engineering solutions and design rules.
- Provide quick access to frequently used technical information.
- Support consistent and efficient switchboard design.

> **Note:** This manual is intended as an internal engineering reference.
